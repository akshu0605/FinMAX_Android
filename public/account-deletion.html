Request deletion of your FinMax account

FinMax users can request deletion of their account and the financial records associated with it.

  1. Send the request from the email address associated with your FinMax account.
  2. Use the subject Delete my FinMax account.
  3. Do not include your password, bank login, or payment credentials.

The developer will verify the request and process account and associated FinMax record deletion in the service systems. You may be contacted if more information is needed to verify account ownership. Any information that must be retained for a legal or security reason will be handled as described in the FinMax Privacy Policy.
