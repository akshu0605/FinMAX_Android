FinMax Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy describes how FinMax, maintained by Akshit Jaswal, handles information when you use the FinMax website or Android app.

Information FinMax handles

  • Account information: email address and profile name from email/password registration or Google sign-in. Supabase Auth handles authentication credentials; FinMax does not receive your Google password.
  • Financial information you enter or import: income, expenses, transaction descriptions, dates, amounts, categories, budgets, reminders, and information you choose to add to shared expense groups.
  • Support messages: information you include when you contact the developer.
  • Technical information: data that may be generated by the hosting, authentication, database, browser, or Android platform services used to operate the app, such as security and diagnostic logs.

How information is used and shared

FinMax uses account and financial information to sign you in, save and display your records, provide budgeting and reporting features, and synchronize your account across supported app surfaces. Google provides the optional Google sign-in service. Supabase provides authentication and database services. Vercel hosts the web app. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

When you choose to use shared expense groups, information you add to a group may be visible to the members you invite or add. FinMax does not sell personal or financial information and does not use it for advertising.

Statement imports

CSV and text-based PDF statements are read and parsed on your device. FinMax saves the transaction rows you choose to import to your account so they can appear in the app. Do not import documents that contain information you do not want stored in your FinMax account.

Storage, security, and retention

Account and financial records are stored using the FinMax Supabase project and are associated with your account. FinMax uses the security controls provided by its platform services and limits app data access to authenticated features. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Records are retained while your account remains active and until a valid deletion request is completed, except for information that must be retained for a legitimate legal or security reason. Contact the developer for questions about a specific record or retention request.

Your choices and account deletion

You can request deletion of your FinMax account and associated records from the app's Settings screen or from the FinMax account deletion page. Deletion requests are reviewed and processed by the developer. The developer may contact you to verify that you control the account. Do not send passwords or bank login credentials in a request.

Children

FinMax is a general personal finance tool and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child provided account information, contact the developer to request its removal.

Contact
